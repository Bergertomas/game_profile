# ADR 0034 — Accepted full Compare direction

**Status:** Accepted · 2026-08-31  
**Context:** ADR 0033; accepted A1–A6; Fable **Should I Play - Canonical
Screens.dc.html** C1–C4 and C-rail; independent candidate review; Tomas's owner
acceptance on 31 August 2026.

## Context

ADR 0033 resolved Compare's URL/index policy and directed a bounded art-led
revision after the first artwork-free candidate proved the truth layer but did
not carry the visual magnetism of the accepted public product.

Fable 5 High produced revised C1–C4 and C-rail. Independent inspection
confirmed that the accepted A1–A6 material remained unchanged, both artwork
territories have balanced perceptual weight, all eight exact paired values and
confidence states remain available, canonical tags are preserved, and the
artless variants retain full comparison meaning. Tomas accepted the revised
result as good enough to proceed.

## Decision

C1–C4 and C-rail are the canonical full Compare visual, information-hierarchy
and interaction direction:

- C1 is the art-led desktop Compare direction;
- C2 is the art-led mobile Compare direction at 390px;
- C3 and C4 are complete desktop/mobile artless-parity directions;
- Compare contains exactly two games and never declares a winner;
- two equal artwork territories establish identity around a central
  two-profile radar without privileging either side;
- the opening makes the clearest difference, strongest alignment and material
  caveat visually distinct before the full instrument;
- all eight exact paired values remain permanently readable and authoritative;
- canonical tags are separated into **Shared**, **left game only** and **right
  game only** without a count, percentage or aggregate;
- game accents identify the two sides; relation words, marker/line structure,
  patterns and exact values carry meaning without dependence on colour or
  brightness;
- range, Unknown, Provisional, asymmetric-confidence, one-art and no-art states
  remain first-class;
- mobile preserves both identities, the trade-off overview and the exact
  instrument in a coherent reading and focus order;
- URL/index behavior remains governed by ADR 0033.

This direction preserves the fixed public 0–10 scale, exactly eight dimensions,
explicit scope and evidence confidence, and the prohibition on public or hidden
aggregate scores, match percentages, normalized shapes, rankings, popularity
or good/bad colour semantics.

## Frozen versus provisional

Frozen are the composition, hierarchy, relationship grammar, two-game artwork
balance, radar role, exact-value authority, canonical tag grouping,
art/artless parity, responsive conceptual order and accessible non-colour
meaning described above.

Still provisional are the specimen artwork and its production rights, exact
asset crops, illustrative copy, specimen destination/time/evidence facts and
the resulting implementation. Shared token, microspacing, motion, state and
accessibility rules are now specified by the completed v1.0 handoff. Production
artwork remains blocked by the seven-step lawful artwork path and per-asset
approval. Acceptance of the design is not production clearance or conformance
proof.

## Consequences

- The dedicated full Compare design gate is closed. Do not rediscover or
  redesign C1–C4 during implementation.
- The first artwork-free candidate remains historical evidence only.
- The shared design-system and interaction handoff across A1–A6 and C1–C4 is
  complete. It reconciles A7's obsolete artwork-free Compare sentence and
  governs measured tokens, component variants, states, responsive behavior,
  focus/keyboard semantics and truth fallbacks.
- Engineering may now begin. The static Search contract stays first in the
  public implementation order, followed by accepted
  homepage/profile slices and then full Compare.
- Visual-conformance and accessibility review follows each relevant
  implementation slice and again before Phase 4 exit.
