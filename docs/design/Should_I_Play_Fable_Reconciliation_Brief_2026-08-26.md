# Should I Play? — Fable Reconciliation Brief

- **Date:** 26 August 2026
- **Mission type:** surgical update of an existing design lock
- **Baseline artifact:** “Should I Play? at Fifteen” / public MVP design-lock artifact
- **Governing decisions:** Master Plan v0.9 plus the 24–25 August public-product decision records

## Mission

Reconcile the existing Fable artifact with the now-frozen public-product
contracts. Do not rediscover the brand, rubric, profile model, or general visual
direction. Preserve what still works, explicitly retire superseded states, and
turn the artifact into an implementation-ready responsive specification for the
12–15-profile private validation corpus and the approximately-100-profile quiet
public release.

The earlier artifact is the visual baseline, not a constraint to preserve a
superseded product hierarchy.

## Frozen foundation — do not reopen

- Public brand is **Should I Play?**; Game Profile is the methodology.
- Exactly eight dimensions; no public or hidden aggregate.
- Low is descriptive; Unknown/range/confidence/scope remain truthful.
- Profile-first Field Guide; no rankings or universal winner.
- D3 remains the individual-profile foundation.
- Field Guide editorial voice plus Registry precision.
- Archivo + Newsreader, graphite + warm paper, ruled hierarchy, game-led accent.
- Avoid generic SaaS, bento, glass, neon gamer HUD, AI storefront, and card soup.
- Compare is exactly two, subordinate to profiles, artwork-free, and noindex at
  pair-result level.
- Compare rows are authoritative; interval relations preserve range/Unknown;
  summary prominence is presentational rather than methodological.
- Mixed art/artless product; artless is a complete state.
- Mobile is a first-class 390px target.

## Product changes since the design lock

### Journey labels and hierarchy

Replace the older Find / Compare / Start with a question model with:

1. **Search** — dominant known-title path;
2. **Compare** — immediately available major secondary action;
3. **What should I play?** — progressive needs-based discovery.

Do not present these as three equal tabs merely because they are three product
capabilities. The homepage remains art-led and utility-first, with compact
authentic artwork supporting the actions rather than a full-height featured-game
hero.

### Catalog scale

- 12–15 profiles is a private/limited validation corpus.
- Approximately 100 substantive profiles is the first credible quiet-public-
  release floor.
- Curation is a navigational/editorial layer over a growing library, not a
  permanent small-catalog strategy.

Designs must therefore prove both states without introducing a new visual
grammar between them.

### Search registry states

Design all four:

1. published profile;
2. recognized but unprofiled, with **Request a Game Profile**;
3. ambiguous title/edition/scope;
4. unrecognized query.

Recognized unprofiled games appear only inside Search; they receive no thin
profile page. No account, public vote count, queue position, or coverage promise.

### What should I play?

This is a bounded deterministic discovery product, not an unrestricted chatbot.
It combines curated entry points, browsing/filters, and ordinary-language input.

The interaction must make interpreted criteria visible and editable. It must
support:

- Must include;
- Prefer;
- Prefer not;
- Must exclude;
- unsupported/unresolved fragments;
- contradictory criteria;
- verified matches;
- matches with trade-offs;
- indeterminate possibilities;
- no verified match and incomplete-catalog states;
- relaxing a hard constraint;
- a small homepage result preview and a durable full-results state;
- earlier transition to full results on mobile.

Do not show a match percentage, hidden quality score, or unrestricted AI
promise. Result explanations identify why a game matched, compromises, and
confidence/provisional context.

### Practical time information

Add two separate profile/discovery concepts:

1. **Total commitment** — public band plus sourced focused/engaged/completionist
   estimates; engaged play governs the headline.
2. **Session suitability** — useful session window plus interruption
   flexibility, with a controlled combined summary.

Design Open-ended, Variable, Unknown, Not applicable, one-axis-unknown, and
conflicting-axis states. Total commitment is not session suitability and neither
is a ninth dimension.

### Individual profile additions

Reconcile placement/hierarchy for:

- Compare with;
- total commitment;
- session suitability;
- supported platforms and the launch “Where to play” action layer;
- editor byline;
- evidence/scope/confidence/cutoff;
- corrections link carrying game/scope context;
- complimentary-access disclosure when present;
- artwork-led and artless states.

Fix the already-settled truth issues in the specification:

- no numeric evidence counts while the ledger is pending;
- no aggregate-like “An 87” example;
- no internal-facing calibration language as the primary public label.

### Compare

Keep the existing interval-relation and artwork-free design lock. Reconcile the
new homepage requirement that a selected pair leads with comparative shape,
then concise interpretation, then a route to full Compare. The interface must
visibly say that a bigger shape is not better.

Design:

- homepage compact pair preview;
- `/compare` launcher;
- full result;
- same-game/different-scope pair;
- exact/range/Unknown combinations;
- asymmetric confidence;
- 390px one-column flow;
- keyboard and screen-reader equivalents;
- artwork-free social card.

### Homepage curation

Preserve:

- objective factual shelves;
- three/four evergreen decision shelves with explicitly authored membership;
- one/two living collections with publication window, expiry, and fallback;
- manually authored “Choosing between…” entries;
- no fake activity, popularity, randomization, or empty padding.

Show how the shelf system grows from 12–15 to approximately 100 profiles without
becoming an endless generic streaming-service carousel.

### Commerce, privacy, and personalization boundaries

- Profiles need an official “Where to play” completion point, with supported
  platform, edition, uncertainty, and affiliate disclosure states.
- Compare does not become a price-comparison surface.
- Discovery-query privacy language should be understandable without dominating
  the interaction.
- Personal matching is post-launch. Show no disabled control or “coming soon.”
