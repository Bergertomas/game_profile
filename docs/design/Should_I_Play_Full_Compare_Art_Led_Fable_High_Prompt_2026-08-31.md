## First-view relationship story

Make these visually striking before the full instrument:

1. **Largest clear difference — Agency & Satisfaction:** Alan Wake 2 7.5
   (High) vs Returnal 10.0 (High).
2. **Exact alignment — Medium-Specific Craft:** 10.0 (High) vs 10.0 (High).
3. **Material caveat:** Alan Wake 2 Execution 9.0 (Medium) vs Returnal 9.5
   (High); Returnal Story and Thematic are Medium confidence because credible
   readings conflict.

Do not render these as three identical bordered report cards. Create one
relationship field or sequence:

- clear difference = separated accent endpoints with a long labelled bridge;
- close = nearby endpoints with a short labelled bridge;
- equal = converged/stacked markers at a luminous neutral midpoint plus the
  explicit word **Equal**;
- indeterminate = no connector and permanent Range/Unknown text.

Use size, brightness, spacing, connector weight, pattern and texture to clarify
the relation. Always retain the game names, exact values, confidence and
relation word. Luminous bone or cyan may identify a meeting point, but shared
does not mean successful and unique does not mean deficient.
