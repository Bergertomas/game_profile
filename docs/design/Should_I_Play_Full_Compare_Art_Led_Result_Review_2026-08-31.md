0033:

- C2 — art-led mobile Compare at 390px;
- C3 — complete artless desktop parity state;
- C4 — complete artless mobile parity state;

  accepted URL/index handoff states.

occupy equal artwork territories and meet around a central two-profile radar.

tag map now separates what the games share from what is distinctive to each.

accessible value groups remain the truth layer. Tomas subsequently accepted
this revised visual and interaction direction; ADR 0034 records the freeze.

## 5. Artwork-balance correction

The initial art-led result still gave Returnal more perceptual weight because
its bright central figure survived the crop while the Alan Wake 2 source/crop
read as a near-black field. A surgical C1/C2-only correction:

- selected the existing Alan Wake 2 project artwork with Alan's lit face and
  red-branch field;
- repositioned and lifted the Alan Wake 2 crop/scrim;
- modestly deepened Returnal's scrim;
- preserved the text-protection gradients, equal territory dimensions, central
  radar position/size, content and interactions.

Visual inspection at the 50% full-stage view now shows both focal subjects with
comparable weight. Neither reads as the chosen game.

## 6. Preserved truth and accessibility

- The radar is still a signature overview rather than the exact authority.
- Eight permanent rows expose exact value and confidence for each game.
- Relation meaning is stated through words and structure as well as colour,
  brightness, connector length and position.
- Equal/shared does not mean better; distinctive does not mean deficient.
- Tags remain controlled descriptors and produce no overlap count or match
  percentage.
- Practical commitment remains Unknown because no approved source record
  exists.
- Art is private design-only. Production remains blocked by per-asset clearance
  and the full lawful-artwork path.

## 7. Remaining caveat

A7 is still provisional shared-system material and contains a historical
sentence saying Compare is artwork-free. ADR 0033, C1–C4 and C-rail supersede
that sentence. Reconcile A7 during the shared design-system/interaction handoff
after Tomas accepts the revised Compare direction; do not treat A7 as current
Compare authority.
