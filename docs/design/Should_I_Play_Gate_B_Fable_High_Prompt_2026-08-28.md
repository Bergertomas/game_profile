## Product identity and methodology

Public product: **Should I Play?**  
Public site: **shouldiplay.gg**  
Evaluation method: **Game Profile**

“Game Profile” is not the public brand.

The product helps someone make a playing decision by showing the shape,
trade-offs, fit and evidence of one game. It has exactly eight dimensions on a
fixed 0–10 scale. There is no public or hidden average, aggregate, area score,
rank, match percentage, winner or universal verdict. A low score is descriptive,
not automatically bad. Unknown is never zero. Scope, build, platform,
confidence and provisional uncertainty remain explicit.

## Design objective

Make the page answer **“Should I play this?”** rapidly, then allow precise
inspection of the eight-dimension method without making the page feel like an
analytical report.

The page must answer in this hierarchy:

1. What game and exact scope is this?
2. What is it like to play?
3. What is the pull, and what tax must I accept?
4. Who is it a strong or poor fit for?
5. How much total time and what kind of session does it ask?
6. What is its full eight-dimension shape and exact values?
7. How confident/current is the evidence?
8. What build/platform differences matter?
9. Where can I play, compare or continue browsing?

The decision answer must outrank the chart. The chart remains the signature
instrument, not the opening obstacle.

## Exact eight-dimension instrument

Use this fixed public order and these exact values. Generate the polygon from
the values; do not trace or reuse an old illustrative shape.

1. Story & Character Investment — **9.5** — High confidence
2. Thematic & Emotional Impact — **9.5** — High confidence
3. Atmosphere & World Pull — **10** — High confidence
4. Medium-Specific Craft — **10** — High confidence
5. Agency & Satisfaction — **7.5** — High confidence
6. Execution & Polish — **9** — Medium confidence
7. Structure & Focus — **8.5** — High confidence
8. Pacing & Time Respect — **8** — High confidence

Use these exact dimension questions in the explanation system:

- Story & Character Investment: How effectively does the game create and
  sustain narrative/character investment on its own terms?
- Thematic & Emotional Impact: How effectively does the game create emotional
  or thematic meaning beyond functional plot progression?
- Atmosphere & World Pull: How strongly does the game create a place, mood and
  sensory identity?
- Medium-Specific Craft: How meaningfully does the game use interactivity itself
  rather than merely delivering content through a game-shaped container?
- Agency & Satisfaction: Does the act of playing create meaningful, satisfying
  action/decision/reward loops?
- Execution & Polish: How reliably and competently does the finished product
  deliver what it is trying to do?
- Structure & Focus: How well is the game's form organized around its intended
  experience?
- Pacing & Time Respect: How well does the game earn the time it asks from the
  player?

The radar is an overview. Pair it with eight permanently visible aligned rows.
Every row shows full label, exact value, confidence text and one concise
plain-language explanation. A real disclosure button may open the fuller
rationale/evidence. Exact values cannot depend on hover, tap or the polygon.

Do not use labels near 9px. Use genuinely readable chart typography, or move
precision to the aligned rows while preserving legible chart labels. Explain
once that a bigger polygon is not a better game. Never show total area.

Show one example open rationale for Execution & Polish on the desktop
interaction state, because its confidence is Medium. Keep the canonical static
screen readable when all rows are collapsed.
