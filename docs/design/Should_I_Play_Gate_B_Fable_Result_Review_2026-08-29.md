## 5. Truth correction applied

The first Fable result claimed a complete play log and claimed that the
practical-time figures had been reconciled against public completion
aggregates. Those statements were not established by the canonical repository
record and conflicted with the explicit design-specimen status.

The A3–A6 trust copy now reads:

> Editor reviewed against the published rubric and scoped evidence record.
> Sources inform the judgment; they are not averaged as votes. See the evidence
> record or report a correction.

The time values remain a layout specimen. Production must replace them with an
approved, scope-aware source record or render Unknown.

## 6. Remaining caveats

### Owner acceptance is complete

Fable's self-check, the independent review and Tomas's owner review establish
the accepted direction. Acceptance authorizes implementation but does not make
specimen time, private artwork or unresolved evidence/destination records true.

### Background verifier did not post

Fable said a background verifier was running after the main pass, but no
separate verifier result appeared. This repeats the known Gate A tooling gap.
The available evidence is Fable's 15/15 self-check plus the independent checks
above. Do not describe the absent verifier as completed.

### A7 is not a shared-system authority

A7 still documents the older token/radius system because it was explicitly
outside the A3–A6 pass. The profile screens follow the governing profile ADRs;
A1/A2 remain the homepage visual authority. Reconcile cross-surface tokens in
the later design-system and interaction handoff. Do not reopen Gate A or
redesign Gate B merely to make A7 internally tidy.

### Illustrative material is not publication truth

- Alan Wake 2 artwork is private design-only and makes no production-rights
  claim.
- Practical time is a labelled specimen.
- Store destinations, platform assets and final evidence links require
  approved production records.
- The canonical dimension values and confidence states come from the repository
  profile; the design does not authorize changes to them.

## 7. Accepted implementation questions

Implementation and conformance review should continue to test these outcomes:

1. Does the opening answer “Should I play this?” quickly enough on desktop and
   mobile?
2. Is Pull/Tax/fit/commitment hierarchy clear without flattening editorial
   voice into a report?
3. Is the full instrument precise and calm enough to inspect?
4. Does the artless state feel equally authored and complete?
5. Are scope, confidence, platform warnings and uncertainty understandable at
   the moment they matter?
6. Does the mobile reading order remain convincing at 390×667 and narrower
   widths/text zoom?

These questions do not reopen the eight dimensions, fixed scale, no-aggregate
rule, Gate A homepage direction or exactly-two artwork-free full Compare
contract.
