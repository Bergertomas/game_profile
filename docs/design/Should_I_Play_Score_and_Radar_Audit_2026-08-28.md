# Should I Play? — Score and Radar Audit

**Date:** 2026-08-28  
**Mode:** read-only methodology, data and visualization audit  
**Purpose:** Gate B input; no score, rubric, content, schema or production-code
change is authorized by this document

## 1. Executive conclusion

Keep the radar as the signature overview, exactly eight dimensions and the
fixed public 0–10 scale. Do not normalize, catalog-rescale, derive an aggregate
or alter a score to create a more dramatic polygon.

The recovered evidence does **not** show that every profile has collapsed into
the same shape. Several profiles are strongly differentiated. It does show two
real calibration weaknesses:

1. **Atmosphere & World Pull** and **Medium-Specific Craft** are top-compressed;
   the current set lacks credible middle and floor anchors for both.
2. **Structure & Focus**, **Pacing & Time Respect** and **Execution & Polish**
   move together often enough that future scoring must police their boundaries
   explicitly rather than treating the relationship as proof that an axis must
   be removed.

The 20-profile matrix is suitable for calibration diagnostics and Gate B
stress-testing. It is not a launch catalog. Only Alan Wake 2, Returnal and
Redfall have canonical published repository profiles. The other 17 totals are
calibration-report values without complete publication packages. The current
Fable example values and polygons are excluded because Gate A explicitly leaves
them unfinished and illustrative.

## 2. Non-negotiable methodology contract

- Public product: **Should I Play?**
- Evaluation construct: **Game Profile**
- Exactly eight dimensions.
- Fixed public scale: 0–10 in half steps.
- Low values are descriptive, not automatically bad.
- Unknown is insufficient evidence, never zero.
- One Unknown subcriterion may yield a visible dimension range; two or more
  yield Not scored under the candidate protocol.
- No public or hidden overall score, average, total area, ranking, winner or fit
  percentage.
- No catalog-relative normalization or rescaling.
- Scope, build, platform, evidence status, confidence and provisional
  uncertainty stay explicit.
- Exact values remain available without relying on hover, shape or color.

Rubric/editorial order is Story, Execution, Structure, Agency, Pacing,
Atmosphere, Thematic, Craft. The fixed public radar order is:

1. Story & Character Investment
2. Thematic & Emotional Impact
3. Atmosphere & World Pull
4. Medium-Specific Craft
5. Agency & Satisfaction
6. Execution & Polish
7. Structure & Focus
8. Pacing & Time Respect

### Shape distinctiveness

Profile-internal standard deviation is highest for The Long Dark — Survival
(1.58), Observer (1.47), Alien: Isolation (1.39), Vampyr (1.17) and Banishers
(1.12). These are demonstrably distinctive silhouettes.

Some flatter polygons are also legitimate:

- Redfall's 4–5.5 band is a coherent broadly weak profile, not a design failure.
- Kingdom Come: Deliverance II and Expedition 33 are very close high profiles
  in the draft matrix. Their similarity requires careful rationale and fit copy,
  not score distortion.
- Banishers and Vampyr legitimately share story/theme strength with
  structure/pacing compromise.
- Spider-Man and Halo are close high-generalist shapes; exact rows and editorial
  interpretation must carry the distinction the polygon cannot.

## 5. Relationship and redundancy audit

### 5.1 Observed correlations

| Pair | Pearson, all 20 | Pearson without Redfall | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Structure / Pacing | 0.879 | 0.840 | Strongest persistent relationship; boundary discipline required |
| Execution / Pacing | 0.851 | 0.806 | General delivery quality and friction can leak into both |
| Execution / Structure | 0.814 | 0.749 | Cohesion/UX/repetition judgments may be double-counted |
| Story / Thematic | 0.797 | 0.743 | Expected in authored narrative games, but constructs remain separable |
| Atmosphere / Craft | 0.834 | 0.651 | Partly driven by high-end selection and general prestige halo |
| Atmosphere / Thematic | 0.821 | 0.675 | Mood can be mistaken for emotional/thematic meaning |
| Thematic / Craft | 0.797 | 0.597 | Mechanics–meaning and lasting impact can share evidence if not mapped carefully |

The correlations are exploratory. Removing each profile's mean score for an
analysis-only shape check reduces Atmosphere/Craft to 0.414 while Story/Thematic
remains 0.534; many all-positive relationships reverse because the general
high/low profile level has been removed. This calculation is **not** a proposed
normalization. It shows that a broad “overall execution/prestige” factor and the
selected corpus explain much of the raw correlation.

Twenty selected profiles cannot establish psychometric redundancy. The current
evidence supports preserving all eight axes and tightening evidence mapping.

### 5.2 Axis-consistency findings

| Dimension | Methodology-defined question | Consistency guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| Story & Character Investment | How effectively does the game create and sustain narrative/character investment on its own terms? | Do not score atmosphere, philosophical depth or production value by themselves. World/lore counts only when it strengthens narrative investment. |
| Execution & Polish | How reliably and competently does the finished product deliver what it is trying to do? | Keep platform/build-specific reliability, implementation and cohesion here. Do not use it as a general quality or enjoyment score. |
| Structure & Focus | How well is the game's form organized around its intended experience? | Own intentional form, information legibility, repetition architecture, UX friction and content focus. Do not re-score the felt passage of time. |
| Agency & Satisfaction | Does playing create meaningful, satisfying action/decision/reward loops? | Low agency may be intentional and descriptive. Do not rescue helpless design because it succeeds elsewhere or duplicate medium-specific meaning. |
| Pacing & Time Respect | How well does the game earn the time it asks? | Own momentum, runtime justification, progress rhythm and density. Do not reward short-session convenience; useful session window is separate practical data. |
| Atmosphere & World Pull | How strongly does the game create a place, mood and sensory identity? | Do not substitute fidelity, general art quality or theme. Memory Residue needs eligible dated retrospective evidence under the candidate protocol. |
| Thematic & Emotional Impact | How effectively does the game create meaning beyond functional plot progression? | Do not award points for topic presence or narrative coherence alone. Lasting Impact needs eligible dated retrospective evidence. |
| Medium-Specific Craft | How meaningfully does the game use interactivity rather than merely delivering content through a game-shaped container? | Apply the passive-medium counterfactual. Do not reuse gameplay quality, polish or ordinary agency as proof. Most excellent games should not automatically reach 9–10. |

The strongest separation cases should remain visible in calibration training:
Observer proves low Agency can coexist with high Atmosphere/Craft; Vampyr proves
rough Execution/Pacing can coexist with high Craft; Alien separates
Atmosphere/Craft from Structure/Pacing/Agency; The Long Dark — Survival proves
low conventional Story need not cap a systemic profile.

### 5.3 Candidate-protocol caution

Protocol v1.0 is still a candidate. Its proposed required-facet minimum affects
six subcriteria across five dimensions and must be tested for systematic
deflation before approval as Rubric v1.1 or rejected. It must not be applied
retroactively to reshape the three published totals. Its confidence derivation,
Unknown/range rules and time-dependent evidence safeguards are nevertheless
valuable Gate B state contracts.

## 7. Radar contract at three levels

### 7.1 Compact homepage fingerprint

**Job:** recognition and orientation—a signature “shape of this game,” not a
precision instrument or quality badge.

- Use the canonical public axis order and fixed 0–10 geometry.
- Keep the compact polygon integrated with the accepted artwork composition.
- Do not show aggregate area, quality color, rank or animated score drama.
- The collapsed fingerprint may omit labels; if exact values are not visually
  adjacent, expanded content must provide an aligned eight-value list.
- When equivalent exact text is present, treat the tiny SVG as decorative for
  assistive technology. Otherwise give it a concise accessible description and
  a route to the values; never attempt to read a path as data.
- State strongest/weakest exact dimensions where space allows, but do not call
  either “best” or “worst.”
- Preserve artwork while Search/Compare/discovery expands.
- Prototype labels near 9px are not production-ready. Use readable type or move
  precision into the aligned list.

### 7.2 Full labelled profile instrument

**Job:** overview plus exact, inspectable methodology.

- Pair one clearly labelled radar with eight exact rows in public radar order.
- Each row exposes full dimension name, exact value or range/Unknown, confidence
  and a concise answer to that dimension's question.
- Explanations/rationales open from the rows, not from hover-only chart points.
- Display overall evidence status, scope/build/platform and evidence cutoff near
  the instrument; dimension confidence stays attached to the affected row.
- Exact, range and Unknown states must differ by structure and text, not color
  alone. Unknown is never plotted at the origin.
- The radar may show a clean uncertainty band/gap only when truthful and
  legible; the rows remain authoritative.
- A keyboard user follows the same logical order as the visual instrument and
  can reach every explanation without focus loss or trapping.
- The page explains once that larger is not universally better and never calls
  the chart a score summary that implies aggregation.

### 7.3 Exactly-two Compare overview

**Job:** reveal different offerings and trade-offs, not choose a winner.

- Keep an optional two-polygon radar as the signature overview, in fixed axis
  order, if validation shows it helps orientation.
- Make the authoritative comparison an artwork-free paired representation:
  exact aligned values/ranges/Unknown for all eight dimensions with non-color
  markers and clear game identities.
- Add a difference-oriented dot/dumbbell layer for dimensions with a clear
  interval relationship. It may lead the detailed Compare reading order, while
  a complete fixed-order eight-row list remains available for consistency.
- Do not midpoint ranges. Equal/intersecting/disjoint/undetermined relationships
  follow the existing interval contract. Confidence changes interpretation, not
  geometry.
- Summaries describe contrast and fit (“more sustained agency,” “less demanding
  pacing”) with scope/confidence qualifiers. They never declare a winner.
- Explain that a bigger polygon is not better; never expose area difference,
  average difference or a hidden preference score.
- Use line style, marker shape, labels and a persistent legend in addition to
  color. Support range/Unknown/asymmetric-confidence cases on mobile and at text
  zoom.

This preserves the radar without asking it to carry precision or accessibility
alone. The full Compare pass may refine the dot/dumbbell treatment after Gate B;
it must not change the eight dimensions or demote the exact rows.

## 8. Gate B design inputs produced by this audit

Use canonical content, not Fable's illustrative values:

- **Primary normal specimen:** Alan Wake 2, because it is published, exact,
  verified, has one Medium-confidence Execution row and a material platform
  warning.
- **Required edge-state references:** Returnal for credible disagreement in
  Story/Thematic confidence; Redfall for provisional status and Low-confidence
  Execution/Structure/Pacing.
- **Shape stress tests:** Observer, Alien, The Long Dark — Survival, Vampyr and
  Banishers as draft-only diagnostic profiles; do not present them as publication
  packages.
- **Range/Unknown specimen:** contract-labelled component/state data until an
  approved real package contains such a result. It must be visibly marked as a
  state specimen, not attributed to a game.

The same normal profile should drive A3/A4 art-led and A5/A6 artless so the
no-art state proves structural parity rather than hiding behind different
content. Provisional, range, Unknown and asymmetric-confidence behavior can be
shown as bounded state variants/annotations within the Gate B handoff without
changing the four canonical page subjects.

## 9. Audit decision

No score change is recommended. Preserve the rubric, eight axes, fixed scale,
confidence/scope truth and no-aggregate rule. Gate B may improve hierarchy,
labels, explanations and comparative precision. It may not manufacture visual
contrast by changing the underlying evaluations.
